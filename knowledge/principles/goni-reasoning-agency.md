# Principal–agent theory

**Diagnostic signal:** Misaligned incentives.

**Core proposition:** Analyze delegation when a principal and agent have different objectives, information, or observability.

**Inputs / assumptions:** Principal, agent, delegated action, effort observability, contract, monitoring and incentive constraints.

**Mathematical or theoretical families:** Incentive compatibility; participation constraint; moral hazard; adverse selection.

**Decision contribution:** Diagnose monitoring costs, hidden action, risk allocation, and accountability.

**Epistemic boundary:** Principal–agent models are institutional analogies for AI systems; LLMs need not possess stable utility functions.

**Representation options:** (1) reusable knowledge-graph reasoning node, (2) prompt/skill with an explicit input-output contract, (3) deterministic mathematical solver when formal inputs are available, or (4) specialized model if matched evaluations justify one. Selection is conditional on task requirements and measured cost/quality.

**Evidence status:** This is a theory-to-problem mapping, not a claim that Goni currently implements or empirically benefits from this framework. Original research citations and benchmarks should be attached through separately reviewed source/evidence nodes.
