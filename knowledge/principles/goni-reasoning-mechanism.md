# Mechanism design

**Diagnostic signal:** Dysfunctional institutional rules.

**Core proposition:** Design rules, incentives, information flows and commitments that induce desirable outcomes under strategic behavior.

**Inputs / assumptions:** Actors, types, feasible allocations, messages, transfers, incentive and participation constraints.

**Mathematical or theoretical families:** Incentive-compatible mechanism design; auctions; matching; social choice.

**Decision contribution:** Specify institutional procedures, decision rights, contracts, and truthful-revelation incentives.

**Epistemic boundary:** Mechanisms require implementability, enforceability, information assumptions, and tests of real behavior.

**Representation options:** (1) reusable knowledge-graph reasoning node, (2) prompt/skill with an explicit input-output contract, (3) deterministic mathematical solver when formal inputs are available, or (4) specialized model if matched evaluations justify one. Selection is conditional on task requirements and measured cost/quality.

**Evidence status:** This is a theory-to-problem mapping, not a claim that Goni currently implements or empirically benefits from this framework. Original research citations and benchmarks should be attached through separately reviewed source/evidence nodes.
