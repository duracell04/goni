# Bounded Decision-Model Landscape Snapshot — 2026-09-23

Recent product infrastructure demonstrates growing interest in model interfaces
optimized for bounded software decisions rather than open-ended language
generation.

The architectural conclusion is intentionally provider-neutral:

state
-> bounded semantic questions
-> typed decisions / predictive scores
-> calibrated selection where evidence permits
-> deterministic software control
-> kernel authorization for effects

This category may reduce the cost of frequent semantic judgments inside agent
and automation loops. It may also be reproduced by task-specific classifiers,
future open models, or generative models exposed through a bounded interface.

Accordingly, current products should be treated as benchmark candidates under
GONI-EXPERIMENT-DECISION-LAYER-01. Their branding, training claims, or
distribution channels do not determine architectural authority.
