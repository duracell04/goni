# Generated versus AI-edited media

## Generated versus AI-edited media

Early labeling created a category error: a real photograph could receive a
"Made with AI" label after a comparatively small AI-assisted edit. Industry
metadata correctly indicated that an AI-capable operation occurred, but the
label could be read as saying the whole image was synthetic.

Meta changed the wording to "AI info" and later refined placement:

- content detected as generated by an AI tool can receive a visible label;
- content detected as only modified or edited by AI tools can place the label
  information in the post's menu; and
- Meta may show whether the basis was an industry-shared signal or
  self-disclosure.

For ads, Meta's 2026 update describes "About this ad" as the three-dot-menu
destination for additional transparency, including AI information detected
from Meta or third-party generative tools. Exact presentation can vary by
content type, significance of the edit, presence of a photorealistic person,
region, and product rollout.
