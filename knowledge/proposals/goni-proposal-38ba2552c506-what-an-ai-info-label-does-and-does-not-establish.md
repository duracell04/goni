# What an "AI info" label does and does not establish

> Status boundary: this is a migrated draft. For `specified_only` nodes, present-tense or enforcement language below states intended contract behavior, not observed implementation, verification, or non-bypassability.

## What an "AI info" label does and does not establish

The label may establish that Meta detected or received a provenance signal
associated with generation or editing. Depending on the signal, it can be
closer to a signed production receipt than to an aesthetic judgment.

The label does not, by itself, establish that:

- every part of the asset was generated;
- the asset is deceptive, inaccurate, or unlawful;
- the asset has never been edited after the recorded action;
- the provenance chain is complete; or
- unlabeled media was made without AI.

Likewise, a missing label is not proof of human-only authorship. Technical
signals may be absent, stripped, unsupported, or below a detector's threshold.
