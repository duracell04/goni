# Jurisdictional exposure of dependencies

Jurisdictional exposure describes the set of legal authorities and obligations relevant to operators, providers, data, software, hardware and authentication or key services associated with a Goni dependency.

It is analytically separate from state sovereignty in public law. Technical ownership does not exempt a person or provider from applicable law.

A dependency profile SHOULD document identifiable providers and responsible operators, asset and processing locations where known, key and identity custody, potential compelled disclosure or service-restriction processes, and lawful contingency arrangements. Competent legal review is necessary for jurisdiction-specific assertions.
