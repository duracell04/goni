## Third brain

The reader-facing name for Goni's active, bounded cognitive responsibilities:
perception, association, retrieval, context assembly, planning, verification,
and action preparation. It maps across the Harness, Context, Control, and
Execution responsibilities and does not own canonical memory, identity, policy,
permissions, approvals, or receipts.
