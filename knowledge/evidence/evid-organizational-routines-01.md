# Organizational routines distinguish generalized patterns from performances

Feldman and Pentland distinguish the abstract or ostensive routine from concrete
performances of that routine.

Goni maps this distinction to two separate objects: execution trajectories record
what happened in individual cases, while WorkflowTemplates represent generalized
procedural hypotheses that may evolve as additional cases are observed.
