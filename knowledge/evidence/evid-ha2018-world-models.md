# Source claim: World Models

Ha and Schmidhuber train a generative model of an environment and show that a
compact controller can be trained using the model's internally generated
environment before being transferred back to the actual environment.

## Goni relevance

The work provides precedent for useful computation over internally generated
states rather than direct external observations alone.

## Boundary

The paper does not validate Goni's memory, authority, receipt, or synthetic
epistemic boundaries. Goni uses the result as a mechanism precedent, not as
evidence that governed offline cognition is safe or useful for personal AI.
