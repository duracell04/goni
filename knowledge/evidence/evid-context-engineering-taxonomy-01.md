# Source claim: context engineering is broader than prompt engineering

Mei et al. treat context engineering as a systems discipline that includes retrieval or generation, processing, and management of the information supplied to models.

For Goni, this supports making RAG one mechanism inside a broader context pipeline rather than a separate maturity stage above or below an agent.
