# Bounded rationality limits exhaustive principal specification

Simon models decision making under cognitive and informational limits rather
than assuming unbounded optimization.

For Goni, the relevant implication is that a principal cannot be expected to
write a complete specification for every future digital contingency. The
system therefore needs compact mandates, policy-level constraints, selective
clarification, and escalation at consequential uncertainty rather than constant
micromanagement.
