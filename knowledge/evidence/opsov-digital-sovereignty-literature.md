# Evidence: plural meanings of digital sovereignty

Couture and Toupin (2019) review divergent sovereignty claims by states and activist collectives. Pohle and Thiel (2020) characterize digital sovereignty as a contested policy discourse.

**Goni interpretation:** define legitimate human delegation, technically effective control and jurisdictional exposure as separate analytic dimensions.

**Evidence limit:** the publications do not assert that Goni implements any such assurance. The operational definition is proposed, not attributed to the authors.
