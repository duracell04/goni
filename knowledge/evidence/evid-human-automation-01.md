# Human-factors research supports function-specific autonomy and calibrated takeover

Parasuraman, Sheridan, and Wickens separate automation across information
acquisition, analysis, decision/action selection, and action implementation.

Lee and See frame trust around appropriate reliance under complexity and
uncertainty. Bainbridge shows that automation can leave humans responsible for
abnormal conditions while weakening the practice and awareness needed for
effective takeover.

For Goni, these findings jointly support granular authority corridors and
escalations that transfer operational state rather than merely announcing that
human input is required.
