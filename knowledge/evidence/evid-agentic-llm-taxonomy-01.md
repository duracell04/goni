# Source claim: agentic LLMs span reasoning, acting, and interacting

Plaat et al. organize the field around three broad capability families: reasoning, acting, and interacting.

This supports a compositional view of agentic systems and cautions against treating RAG, tool use, or multi-agent interaction as successive generations of one architecture.
