# Case evidence: Crypto AG and supplier trust

The Swiss parliamentary supervisory delegation investigated Crypto AG and its relationship with foreign intelligence services; it published a report dated 2 November 2020.

**Relevance:** product cryptographic strength must be assessed alongside implementation and supplier integrity.

**Limit:** the incident illustrates a supply-chain threat class, not a Goni-specific technical outcome.
