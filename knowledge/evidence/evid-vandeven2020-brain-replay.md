# Source claim: Brain-inspired replay

The paper studies replay of generated internal representations as a mechanism
for retaining previously learned capabilities under continual learning.

## Goni relevance

It provides technical precedent for selective replay and internal
reconstruction as useful offline computation.

## Boundary

DREAM-01 does not infer a biological sleep architecture from this result and
does not require online weight training. The source does not validate synthetic
memory as factual evidence.
