# Case evidence: Salt Typhoon telecom compromise

The FBI's public statement of 24 April 2025 describes Salt Typhoon's compromise of multiple US telecom companies, resulting in theft of call data logs, a limited number of private communications and selected material subject to court-ordered requests.

**Relevance:** communications networks constitute external threat boundaries, including metadata exposure.

**Limit:** this infrastructure compromise differs technically from manipulated supplier products and service-provider account controls.
