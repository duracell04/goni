# Case evidence: disputed ICC email suspension allegations

In its 8 July 2025 answer to House of Lords question HL8755, the UK government acknowledged reports that Microsoft may have blocked ICC Chief Prosecutor Karim Khan's email account following US sanctions and stated that Microsoft **strongly denied** having done so.

**Relevance:** an externally administered account introduces potential service-continuity and jurisdictional dependencies.

**Evidence limit:** the source proves that the allegation and denial were made. It does **not** establish that Microsoft suspended the account.
