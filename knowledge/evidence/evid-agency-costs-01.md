# Delegation creates governance and monitoring costs

Classical agency theory provides a useful structural lens for delegated AI: specification, monitoring, verification, residual error, and governance all consume resources. Goni adopts that cost lens without importing the assumption that artificial delegates possess human preferences or legal personality.
